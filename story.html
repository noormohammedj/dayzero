Our story

It started with kids. It turned into thedayzero.

Before there was a ₹99 Quick Start or a 30-day course, there was a simpler problem we couldn't stop thinking about: almost nobody — kids or adults — is ever asked what they actually want to build. They're handed a short list of "safe" answers and told to pick one.

It started with kids

Our parent venture, TheKahaaniya, runs immersive storytelling events for kids — built on one belief: children should be free to dream past "doctor" or "engineer" into whatever they're actually curious about. Watching kids light up the moment someone finally asked them "but what do you want to build?" — instead of handing them a script — was the spark.

It didn't take long to notice the exact same thing happening to adults. Most people never get asked that question either. They pick a job, or a business idea, the same way they were handed "doctor or engineer" as kids — because it's safe, not because it's theirs. thedayzero exists to ask adults that same question, properly, and then help them act on the answer.
