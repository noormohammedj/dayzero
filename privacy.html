Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

thedayzero.in is run by Noor Mohammed J (RoJerU), Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India. This page explains what we collect when you visit the site, sign up for a free session, or buy a course, why we collect it, and how you can ask us to change or delete it. "We" and "us" mean Noor Mohammed J.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We use these services to run the site, and they process data for us under their own privacy terms:

  • Razorpay for payments and refunds.
  • Google for Google Analytics, and for Google Sheets and Apps Script, where form entries and book access are kept.
  • Meta for the Meta Pixel (Facebook and Instagram ads) and WhatsApp, where we talk to you.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the website and may log basic request data such as your IP address.
  • Google Meet for live sessions you register for.

We may also share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Payment records are kept as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires. Form entries and chat history are kept while they are useful for helping you, and deleted when you ask. Analytics data follows Google's and Meta's retention settings.

Your choices and rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can withdraw your consent to being contacted at any time. Reply "stop" on WhatsApp and we will stop messaging you. To make any request, contact us using the details below. We aim to reply within 7 days.

You can block analytics and ad cookies with your browser's settings or an ad blocker. The site still works without them.

Children

Our paid courses are for people aged 18 and over. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to buy for you.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
