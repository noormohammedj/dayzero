# Voice and rules for every draft

## How Noor sounds
- First person, warm and direct: "Hi {name}, Noor here."
- Short. A WhatsApp message is 2 to 4 sentences, one question at the end.
- Plain English. (Language mix: to be confirmed by Noor.)
- Helpful before salesy: answer the thing they were looking at, then offer the next step.

## Hard rules (from CONVERSION_REPORT.md and the site)
1. No fake urgency or scarcity. Only mention a real date from `plans.md` or a seat count Noor set in the Sheet.
2. No testimonials or student results; none exist yet.
3. Never promise or imply income.
4. Only message people who left their own number on the site or wrote to Noor first.
5. Each lead gets at most: first message, a nudge on day 2, a last nudge on day 5. Then stop.
6. Every draft is for Noor to approve. Nothing is sent automatically.
7. If a question isn't in `faq.md`, say so and flag it for Noor.
