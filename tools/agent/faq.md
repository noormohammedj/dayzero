Support drafts answer only from this file and `plans.md`. If a question isn't covered, the desk flags it for Noor instead of guessing. When Noor answers a new question, add it here.

**Why is it ₹1,999 right now?**
The course fee is ₹4,999. It is ₹1,999 until 30 November 2026, then it goes back to ₹4,999.

**Can I really earn from this?**
You can use the skill two ways: build websites for clients as a freelancer (Indian freelancers typically charge ₹5,000–₹20,000 for a one-page site), or sell a digital product of your own. The OS shows both. It doesn't promise income; that depends on the work you put in.

**Why pay ₹1,999 when an AI subscription costs about the same?**
An AI subscription gives you the tool, every month. The OS is a one-time playbook: what to ask, in what order, with 154 tested prompts and the steps behind three live products. Most of it works on the free Claude and ChatGPT plans, and if it isn't for you, you get a full refund within 7 days.

**Do I need to know how to code?**
No. The book teaches you to describe what you want clearly, test it on a real phone and report what broke. Claude writes the code. If you've worked in IT as a PM, QA, BA or tech lead, you already have the skills that matter.

**Is this a PDF?**
It's a web book you read in your browser, built for phones. You can save the whole book as a PDF at any time with one button.

**How do I get access after paying?**
Right after payment, Razorpay sends you to the reader and the book unlocks automatically. On a new device, unlock it again with the email and phone number you used to pay.

**What if I'm not satisfied?**
Message Noor on WhatsApp within 7 days of payment and you get your full ₹1,999 back to your original payment method through Razorpay. No questions asked, no forms.

**Does it promise income?**
No. It teaches a method and shows real numbers from three products, including the ones that didn't sell. Nobody honest can promise income.

**Which tools do I need, and what do they cost?**
Claude, ChatGPT and Canva (free plans are enough to start), a domain name, a free GitHub account and a Razorpay account. Chapter 3 prices a first launch line by line.

**What happens when Claude or ChatGPT change?**
Because it's a web book, chapters are updated in place when the tools change. Your access includes those updates.
