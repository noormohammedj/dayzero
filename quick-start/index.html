Step 1 · Find What's Already Yours

You already have the raw material.

This is the Ikigai method — a simple way of finding what sits at the overlap of four things: what you love, what you're good at, what the world needs, and what someone would pay for. Answer honestly, in your own words. There's no wrong answer here, only an honest one.

1
Think about what you do on a free Sunday, without being asked.
2
What do people keep asking you for help with?
3
Something people complain about, or wish existed.
4
Not your dream job. Just something real, right now.
Your Zone, So Far
Fill in all four to see where they overlap.
Step 2 · Day 0–3

Foundation.

Get clear before you build. Before any post, page, or product — get honest clarity on what you're building and for whom. Tick each box as you go; your answers are saved as you type.

DAY 0

Set Your Why

One honest reason, and a name you can say out loud.

Write one honest line: why are you starting this business?
Choose a simple business name and save it across your phone.
DAY 1

Know Your One Customer

Not everyone. One real person.

Describe one real person who would buy from you — age, life stage, need.
Note where they spend time, and study three pages you admire.
DAY 2–3

Get Offer Clarity

One offer, priced on purpose — not guessed.

List everything you could sell, then narrow it to your top one or two offers.
Price it: your cost + your time + a fair margin — not a guess.
Finish this line: "People should choose me because ___"
