Questions

Before you pay.

I'm not in India. Can I join?
Yes. Everything is online, in English, on Google Meet. Prices are charged in Indian rupees and Razorpay accepts international cards; your bank converts the amount. Session times are shown in your own time zone on this page.
Do I need to know coding?
No. You describe what you want in plain English and Claude or ChatGPT writes it. I built the Plotskart app this way, with Claude Code.
Which one should I start with?
If you've never built anything with AI, start with the ₹199 crash course. If you want the full system to work through on your own, take the OS. If you want your product live in four weeks with me beside you, choose 1:1.
How do I book my 1:1 sessions?
After you pay, Razorpay takes you to my booking page. Pick four slots on Saturdays or Sundays between 11 AM and 4 PM IST. You get a calendar invite with the Google Meet link for each one.
Can you promise I'll earn what you earn?
No. My numbers took time and many weekends. I can teach you the method and help you build. What you earn depends on what you build and how much you sell it.
What if it's not for me?
The Weekend Founder OS has a 7-day full refund: message me on WhatsApp within 7 days of paying. For the crash course, message me before it starts if you can't make it.
