16 years into a career in IT at EY, and for most of them, something felt off — like I was watching my own life from a distance instead of building it. So on the side, in the hours before and after work, I started building anyway.

This book is not theory I read somewhere. It's the exact sequence I followed, written down day by day, by someone who is still doing the 9-to-6 while doing this too.
