Four phases. Each one builds on the last.

No slides about "the future of AI". You open Claude or ChatGPT at the start and keep it open the whole time.

10 – 10 min

Set up

Open a free Claude or ChatGPT account and fill a 5-line notes template about yourself. That's your raw material.

210 – 45 min

Build

345 – 70 min

Polish

470 – 90 min
