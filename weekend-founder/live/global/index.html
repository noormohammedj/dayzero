Four kinds of people. One evening's work.

The business owner

Your customers find you on WhatsApp and word of mouth. You've been quoted hundreds for a website and still don't have one.

The freelancer or creator

Your work lives in Instagram posts and Drive links. You want one clean page to send clients that says who you are.

03 · The 60 minutes

Four phases. Each one builds on the last.

No slides about "the future of AI". You open Claude or ChatGPT at the start and keep it open the whole time.

10 – 10 min

Set up

Open a free Claude or ChatGPT account and fill a 5-line notes template about yourself. That's your raw material.

210 – 30 min

Build

One well-written prompt turns your notes into a full website. You open it in your browser and see it working.

330 – 45 min

Polish

Make it look premium, fix it on your phone, add WhatsApp and email buttons. You learn how to correct AI, not just ask it.

445 – 60 min

Sell it

How to price a site, find your first local client and pitch them this week. Then Q&A, and the Weekend Founder Cohort for people who want to do this with me over four Saturdays.
