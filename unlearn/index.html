Discovery

Why are you really doing this?

Are you building this because it's genuinely who you are — or because you needed the income, someone pushed you into it, you had no other option, or you're still chasing someone else's approval? There's no wrong answer. There's just an honest one.

Sit with that question for a second before you turn the page.

Rewire

Unlearn what you were taught to build.

Before: you built a business to survive, to prove something, because somebody told you it was the smart move. After: your business becomes an expression of who you actually are — not a performance for anyone watching.

Unlearning is the bridge between the two.

MOVEMENT 3 · 1:15–1:45
13 / 17
Rewire In 3 Moves

Small moves. Real shift.

1
Name the "should"
Write down one thing in your business you're doing only because you felt you were supposed to
2
Trace it to your Ikigai sheet
Does it sit inside any of your four circles — or outside all of them?
3
Rewrite one decision
Pick one thing you'll change in your business this month, on purpose

Pair up. Say your "should" out loud — out loud is where unlearning actually starts.
